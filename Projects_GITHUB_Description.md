**AstroSage Call Center Performance Analysis | Excel Project**

---

Analyzed **28,027 call and chat consultation records** from AstroSage — one of the world's largest astrology platforms — to uncover operational inefficiencies and guide a **₹1 Crore strategic investment decision**.

The project involved end-to-end data analytics: cleaning a 35-attribute raw dataset, performing exploratory and diagnostic analysis using **Excel (Pivot Tables, CORREL function, aggregation formulas)**, and building an interactive **dashboard** to present insights to stakeholders.

**Key findings included:**

* Only **40.6% of calls** and **28.4% of chats** were successfully completed, signaling critical technology gaps  
* **57.35% of all calls were repeat calls**, indicating a low First Call Resolution (FCR) rate  
* **Calls drove \~79% of total revenue**, making call completion rate a top business priority  
* A near-zero **correlation (0.055)** between call duration and customer satisfaction proved quality matters more than duration  
* Severe **workload imbalance** — top guru handled 1,580 consultations while bottom 10 combined handled only 24  
* Customer ratings were heavily skewed toward 0–3 (out of 8), with only top 5 gurus averaging above 5.8

**Strategic Budget Recommendation (₹1 Crore):**

* 30% → Structured Training Programs  
* 25% → Technology Upgrade (CRM, Chatbots, Callback System)  
* 25% → Business Expansion & Digital Marketing  
* 10% → Performance-Based Incentives  
* 10% → Contingency / Pilot Programs

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel,Google Sheets,Google Slides,Google Docs
